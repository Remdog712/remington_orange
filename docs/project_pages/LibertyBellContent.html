Liberty Bell Content Creation

Blender renders, motion sequences, and promotional edits built from the bell scan

View Scan Project Visit VAR Project Page

The July 4 release video, which also supplied the background motion used on the VAR Lab Liberty Bell project page.

After the Liberty Bell scan was processed, I created a second wave of public-facing content built around the finished model. That meant turning a technical cultural heritage capture into images, teaser videos, and a release piece that could communicate the project quickly and visually for the VAR Lab website and social media.

I made the video content in Blender, where I set up the bell, cameras, lighting, and render passes around the cleaned scan data. From there, I built motion sequences and composited supporting elements in Adobe After Effects, then handled final editing, pacing, polishing, and export in Adobe Premiere Pro. The still images on this page were also created in Blender, using the processed Liberty Bell model as the foundation for material studies, mesh views, cutaway graphics, and environmental presentation renders.

These pieces helped extend the scan beyond documentation by giving the public multiple ways to experience the project: as a patriotic release video, quick teaser edits, stylized stills, and process visuals that show how the scan transformed from capture data into a polished digital artifact.
