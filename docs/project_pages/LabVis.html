LabVis Research Videos

I created a set of research explainer videos for Bibiana Mayer Steckle, a researcher and PhD student at UFCSPA in Brazil, for her project LabVis. The goal was to turn research ideas into short, clear videos that could work for both English- and Portuguese-speaking audiences.

I built the motion graphics and edits in Adobe After Effects and Adobe Premiere Pro, shaping each piece to feel readable, polished, and easy to follow. I produced English and Portuguese versions so the content could travel across audiences without losing clarity or pacing.

I also used ElevenLabs-generated voices as part of the narration workflow, which helped keep delivery consistent across languages while staying flexible in post-production.
